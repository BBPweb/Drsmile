INVISALIGN CLEAR ALIGNERS

Straighter teeth, without anyone noticing

Wires and brackets are no longer the only way to align your teeth. Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom-made trays that shift your teeth a little at a time. You can take them out to eat, brush and floss, and they are hard to spot in photos and meetings. Your plan starts with a quick digital scan instead of messy moulds, so you can preview the result and start with confidence.
