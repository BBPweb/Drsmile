01Where are your clinics located?

Dr. Smile has clinics in Kalamassery and Pannampilly Nagar, Kochi, Kerala. You can find a clinic on the map, or contact our team to confirm the location for your appointment.

02How can I book an appointment?

Call +91 484 298 5200 or email info@drsmile.co.in. Let our team know your preferred clinic and the reason for your visit, so they can help you arrange an appointment that suits you.

03What should I bring to my first visit?

If you have previous dental records or scans, bring them along. It also helps to write down your questions and priorities, so nothing is forgotten during your consultation. When booking, ask our team whether anything else is needed for your appointment.

04Can I discuss my concerns before treatment?

Yes. Your questions, comfort and priorities are an important part of the conversation. Share what you would like to understand, and ask your specialist to explain the options and next steps before you decide.

05Which dental specialties can I explore?

Our website covers orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, digital dentistry, implant dentistry, endodontics, periodontics, pediatric dentistry, oral surgery and prosthodontics. Explore our services, then contact the team about the care you are looking for.

06How will I understand my treatment plan?

Our approach centres on listening, explaining your options and planning together. During your consultation, ask about the proposed steps, appointments and anything you would like clarified. Learn about our approach.

07Where can I ask about costs and appointment availability?

Contact our clinic team for current appointment availability. For treatment costs, ask for an estimate based on your consultation and proposed care plan, including what is covered in the estimate.

08Where can I learn about braces and clear aligners?

Visit our orthodontics page to explore braces and clear aligners. Your consultation is the place to discuss your smile goals and ask which options your specialist would consider for you.

09What does the aligner journey look like?

Our aligner journey starts with a consultation and assessment, followed by a digital plan and preview, then custom aligners. Your specialist can explain how these stages would apply to your individual plan. See the journey.

10Can I ask about a digital smile preview?

Yes. Ask your specialist about digital planning and whether a preview is part of your proposed treatment. You can also explore our digital dentistry page before your visit.
