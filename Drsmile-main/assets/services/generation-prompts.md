## braces-orthodontics.png

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: PNG illustration for a premium dental clinic website service card, designed to read clearly when displayed about 240 by 170 pixels.
Style/medium: polished realistic 3D product render with softly rounded details, subtle warm ivory enamel, muted blush gums where needed, refined champagne gold accents and brushed silver dental hardware. Tasteful clinical accuracy, welcoming and clean.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with alpha, isolated object cutout; no backdrop, no floor, no rectangular tile, no cast shadow rectangle.
Composition/framing: one centered cohesive subject, slight three-quarter perspective, square canvas, complete object within frame, generous 10 percent transparent edge padding, subject occupies roughly 80 percent of width. Soft studio light from upper left, delicate contact shading only within the object.
Constraints: absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no border, no hands, no people, no faces, no blood, no excessive sparkles. Output PNG.
Primary request: A small upper and lower dental arch model with neat white teeth and discreet silver orthodontic brackets joined by a continuous archwire. Soft muted blush gingiva on the model, paired arches slightly apart, frontal three-quarter view. Minimal, premium dental service illustration for Braces and Orthodontics.
